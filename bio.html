Bio

Where Jazz, Games, and Techno Meet

For me, music starts long before I open a DAW or play a note. Sometimes it begins with a feeling, a scene from a game, a melody that won't leave my head, or a rhythm I hear while walking through the day.

I started as a jazz pianist, where I learned to listen, improvise, and find unexpected connections between notes. That foundation still shapes everything I create today, even though my music has moved into the world of electronic production.

As a lifelong gamer and fan of electronic music, I'm fascinated by the way sound can create atmosphere, emotion, and movement. I enjoy building tracks the same way a designer builds an experience, layer by layer, idea by idea, until every sound has a purpose.

The collection on this website is where jazz, electronic music, storytelling, and curiosity influences come together. Every track I produce is an opportunity to explore a new idea and turn it into something people can feel.
